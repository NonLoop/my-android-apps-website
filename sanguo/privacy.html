隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日｜适用应用：三国象棋（Android，包名 com.oneusop.sanguo）

一、总则

本应用由个人开发者（下称“我们”）发布。我们不设账号体系，不要求登录，不收集你的通讯录、短信、精确位置、照片或任何文件。你在本应用中的设置、对局进度与偏好仅保存在你的设备本地。

二、我们直接处理的信息

  • 本地设置：语言、音效、触感、棋子外观、难度、是否已去广告等，保存在设备本地存储，卸载即删除。
  • 对局数据：棋谱与局面只存在于运行内存中，不落盘、不上传。

以上信息不会离开你的设备，我们无法读取。

三、第三方 SDK 处理的信息

Google AdMob（广告）

免费版展示横幅、插屏与激励视频广告。AdMob 可能收集并处理：广告标识符（Advertising ID）、IP 地址、设备型号与操作系统版本、粗略位置（由 IP 推断）、广告展示与点击记录、崩溃与性能诊断信息。用途为投放与衡量广告、防止欺诈。详见 Google 隐私政策 与 AdMob 数据披露。

应用内购买

本应用不提供任何付费内购。免广告时长通过自愿观看激励视频获得，观看次数与到期时间仅保存在本机，不上传服务器。

除上述 SDK 外，本应用不集成其他广告、统计或社交 SDK；不向第三方出售、出租或交换个人信息。

四、权限说明

  • INTERNET、ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE：加载广告。
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID：读取广告标识符，用于广告投放与频次控制。

本应用不申请位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录、存储读写等权限。对局 AI 完全运行在本地，飞行模式下可正常游玩。

五、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。若你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系，我们会尽快删除。

六、数据保留与删除

我们不运营服务器，不保存你的任何数据。删除本应用即删除全部本地数据。若要限制广告个性化，可在系统“设置 → Google → 广告”中重置或删除广告 ID；免广告记录仅保存在本机，卸载即清除。

七、你的权利

由于我们不持有你的个人数据，通常无法为你导出或删除服务器端数据。对 AdMob 所处理的数据，可通过上述 Google 链接行使权利。

八、政策更新

若功能或第三方 SDK 发生变化，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。重大变更会在应用内提示。

1. Overview

This app is published by an individual developer (“we”). There is no account system and no sign-in. We do not collect your contacts, messages, precise location, photos or files. Your settings and game preferences are stored only on your device.

2. Information we handle directly

  • Local settings: language, sound, haptics, piece style, difficulty and whether ads have been removed, stored in the device’s local storage and erased on uninstall.
  • Game data: board positions and move history live in memory only; they are never written to disk or uploaded.

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

3. Information processed by third-party SDKs

Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS version, coarse location inferred from IP, ad impression and click events, and crash/performance diagnostics, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure.

In-app purchases

This app has no paid in-app purchases. Ad-free time is earned by voluntarily watching rewarded videos; the view count and expiry are stored only on your device and never uploaded.

No other advertising, analytics or social SDKs are integrated. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: load ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: read the advertising ID for ad delivery and frequency capping.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions are requested. The AI runs entirely on-device; the game is fully playable in airplane mode.

5. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

6. Retention and deletion

We operate no servers and store none of your data. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. To limit ad personalization, reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. Ad-free records are stored only on your device and cleared on uninstall.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on servers, we generally cannot export or delete server-side data for you. For data processed by AdMob, please use the Google links above.

8. Changes

If features or SDKs change, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be flagged in the app.
