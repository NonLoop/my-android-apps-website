隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 22 日｜适用应用：蓝色记账（iOS / Android，包名 com.luohuidan.jizhang）

一、总则

本应用由个人开发者（下称“我们”）发布。我们不设账号体系，不要求注册登录，不提供云端同步，不收集你的通讯录、短信、精确位置或任何文件。你的账目数据仅保存在设备本地。

二、我们直接处理的信息

  • 账目数据：收入、支出、分类、备注、预算与设置等，保存在设备本地存储中，不上传任何服务器。卸载本应用即一并删除。
  • 备份与导出：仅在你想用时由你主动发起，文件保存在你选择的位置，我们不接收副本。

以上信息不会离开你的设备，我们无法读取。

三、第三方 SDK 处理的信息

Google AdMob（广告）

免费版展示插屏广告（仅低概率出现）与激励视频广告。AdMob 可能收集并处理：广告标识符（Advertising ID）、IP 地址、设备型号与操作系统版本、粗略位置（由 IP 推断）、广告展示与点击记录、崩溃与性能诊断信息。用途为投放与衡量广告、防止欺诈。详见 Google 隐私政策 与 AdMob 数据披露。购买 Pro 后不再展示任何广告。

应用内购买（Pro 高级版）

Pro 为一次性买断（非订阅），由 Apple App Store / Google Play 处理支付。我们无法访问你的支付信息，购买状态由应用商店管理，可通过商店的“恢复购买”还原。

除上述 SDK 外，本应用不集成其他广告、统计或社交 SDK；不向第三方出售、出租或交换个人信息。

四、权限说明

  • INTERNET、ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE：加载广告。
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID：读取广告标识符，用于广告投放与频次控制。
  • 计费权限（com.android.vending.BILLING）：完成 Pro 买断购买。

本应用不申请位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录等权限。记账功能完全运行在本地，飞行模式下可以正常记账。

五、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向收集个人信息，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。若你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系，我们会尽快删除。

六、数据保留与删除

我们不运营服务器，不保存你的任何数据。删除本应用即删除全部本地数据（包括账目与设置）。若要限制广告个性化，可在系统“设置 → Google → 广告”中重置或删除广告 ID。

七、你的权利

由于我们不持有你的个人数据，通常无法为你导出或删除服务器端数据。对 AdMob 所处理的数据，可通过上述 Google 链接行使权利。

八、政策更新

若功能或第三方 SDK 发生变化，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。重大变更会在应用内提示。

1. Overview

This app is published by an individual developer (“we”). There is no account system, no sign-up and no cloud sync. We do not collect your contacts, messages, precise location or files. Your ledger data is stored only on your device.

2. Information we handle directly

  • Ledger data: income, expenses, categories, notes, budgets and settings, stored in the device’s local storage and never uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Backup & export: initiated only by you, saved where you choose. We never receive a copy.

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

3. Information processed by third-party SDKs

Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version shows interstitial ads (shown rarely) and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS version, coarse location inferred from IP, ad impression and click events, and crash/performance diagnostics, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure. No ads are shown after buying Pro.

In-app purchase (Pro)

Pro is a one-time, non-subscription purchase processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play. We never see your payment details; purchase state is managed by the store and can be restored via “Restore Purchases”.

No other advertising, analytics or social SDKs are integrated. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: load ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: read the advertising ID for ad delivery and frequency capping.
  • Billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING): complete the Pro purchase.

No location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions are requested. Bookkeeping runs entirely on-device; the app is fully usable in airplane mode.

5. Children’s privacy

The app does not direct data collection at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

6. Retention and deletion

We operate no servers and store none of your data. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data, including ledger entries and settings. To limit ad personalization, reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on servers, we generally cannot export or delete server-side data for you. For data processed by AdMob, please use the Google links above.

8. Changes

If features or SDKs change, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be flagged in the app.
